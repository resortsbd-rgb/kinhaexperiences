A story born in the jungle

Kin-Ha is part of the living history of the Route of the Cenotes.

Before becoming a meeting place for travelers, Kin-Ha grew from a local family’s bond with the water, stone and jungle of Puerto Morelos. In time, this remarkable landscape opened its doors so more people could experience it with respect.

Today, we want every visit to leave behind more than a photograph: curiosity, gratitude and a desire to help protect the natural world that made the experience possible.

Ancient water. Living stone. A jungle that endures.

Selva Guardian

To experience this place is also to learn how to care for it.

Selva Guardian is a private, voluntary initiative by Cenotes Kin-Ha created to support practical environmental care. Its purpose is simple: connect visitors and partners with projects that can be clearly explained, documented and carried out responsibly.

Our approach

Conservation without empty promises.

We would rather promise less and deliver more. Every initiative must have a clear purpose and a scope we can responsibly sustain.
